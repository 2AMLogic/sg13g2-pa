## Tool check (answered from the runs)

1. **Does the campaign sample non-zero variation on `npn13G2`, per instance?** Yes, from completed draws: under `hbt_typ_mismatch` the 14 identically biased output devices differ **within a single draw** (per-draw max-min across the array: mean 0.0002298 A, sample sigma 4.86e-05 A) and the same-draw difference `ic1-ic2` moves across draws (sigma 0.0001016 A), while under `hbt_typ_stat` the per-instance spread within a draw is exactly 0 A (every device moves together; the process section draws one global multiplier set per die) and `ic1-ic2` sigma is 1.631e-18 A. The diode-connected reference moves too (see below) -- common movement across draws can be process or the shared mirror bias; the per-instance spread within a draw is what only mismatch produces.

2. **Can a deterministic negative control be declared for this family (scaled or gated mismatch)?** No, not through `klt` at the pinned 0.9.0 client:
   - **scaled control** (`probe_scale.request.json`): refused before any draw, verbatim: `request.monte_carlo.mismatch_scale is only supported for PDK families with a verified multiplicative mismatch switch (gf180mcu, sky130); models.pdk 'ihp-sg13g2' resolves to family 'sg13g2'` -- 2AMLogic/klayout-tools#5486.
   - **gated control** (`probe_instances.request.json`): refused before any draw, verbatim: `request.monte_carlo.instances needs models.pdk naming a PDK with a known mismatch switch (gf180mcu, sky130); got 'ihp-sg13g2'` -- 2AMLogic/klayout-tools#5487.
   The card's own gate is the subcircuit parameter `mm_ok` (default 1); the pinned client cannot reach it (`mismatch_scale`/`instances` shadow a global `.param` switch, which sg13g2 does not have). The two-family curation and the `mm_ok` route are 2AMLogic/klayout-tools#5486; the sg13g2 `instances` work landed upstream only after the 0.9.0 pin (2AMLogic/klayout-tools#5487, completed 2026-11-15), and the measured two-point scaling check that acceptance still wants is 2AMLogic/klayout-tools#5503. The `vary: "process"` axis is NOT a control for this deck family either: klt feeds ngspice one combined `.options seed=`, so an agauss deck re-rolls under any axis (klt's own `_expand_monte_carlo` docstring says the control does not extend to such decks).

3. **Does the request run on the batch fleet with the pinned client (`sim/toolchain.json` klt 0.9.0)?** Yes: every run above resolved backend `batch` through the 0.9.0 client (job ids in the table; the runner is the fleet's 0.9.0).

## Limits of this record

- n=100 per run: a sigma estimate carries roughly +/-10 % (sigma of a sigma estimate ~ sigma/sqrt(2n)); enough to size a campaign, not a yield statement. No quantile here is a spec limit.
- The passives and the bias source are ideal: the resistor/capacitor statistical sections of the PDK reach nothing in this netlist. Only the 15 HBTs are PDK devices.
- The AC deck carries a second DUT copy (X2, reverse excitation); its devices also draw mismatch, but S21/S11 above read the forward copy X1 only.
- Self-heating is on (card default); the operating point is the DUT's own mirror.
- No negative control completed at this pin: a zero-sigma row here would be structural (the stat section's within-draw spread), not a declared control (2AMLogic/klayout-tools#5486).
