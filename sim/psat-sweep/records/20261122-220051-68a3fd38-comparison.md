## Matched collector load

This table supersedes the collector-load figures in the
[stabilised matched record](20261121-094224-e6159f8-match.md). The external
termination is `Lmatch + (Cmatch || 50 Ω)` in impedance notation.
The design target adds the series `Cblock` to that external impedance.
The actual collector load includes `Rsho` in parallel at RFOUT, then
`Cblock` in series, then `Rdchoke` and `Lchoke` in parallel at COLL
with VCC at AC ground. Values below use the unrounded ideal element values
from the [matched deck](../psat_sweep_match.spice). These are circuit

## Device stress

Peak voltages below are independent maxima over each sweep. The process
minima are 1.4 V for BVCEO and 3.8 V for BVCBO. Each sweep also has samples
outside the VBIC voltage window. The voltage and temperature samples are
independent of the collector-current calculation. [Device limits](../../lib/device_limits.py)

| Sweep | Peak VCE (V) | Peak VCB (V) | Voltage-based findings |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Bare, before | 1.476 | 1.442 | BVCEO exceeded, outside model window |
| Bare, after | 1.387 | 0.893 | Within both breakdown minima, outside model window |
| Matched, before | 5.000 | 4.527 | BVCEO and BVCBO exceeded, outside model window |
| Matched, after | 3.689 | 3.048 | BVCEO exceeded, outside model window |

The matched sweep after stabilisation already exceeds BVCEO at Psat
(3.616 V peak VCE at +9.6 dBm available). Its highest VCE occurs at +10 dBm.
For both post-change benches the per-device current is
`[i(Lchoke) + (v(VCC) − v(COLL))/Rdchoke − i(boundary) − v(RFOUT)/Rsho] / 14`.
The archived post-change current peaks and current-window fractions used the
previous reducer. A corrected stress table requires the time-aligned branch
waveforms from a fresh fleet sweep.
