### Cause of the pre-#172 instability, and what changed

Before the issue-#172 network (`records/20261118-225926-ef3c005.md`, same cell), mu was 0.0228 at 0.176 GHz and below 1 at **every** one of the 174 points. The cause is circuit-level: the array idles with V_CE = 0.8 - V_BE ≈ 22 mV (the draft supply cannot support 2 V_BE), so the BC junction is weakly forward-biased and reverse transmission through the device is only ~-25 dB; with 17-29 dB of forward gain below the band, the loop product |S12*S21| ≈ 0.7 from 60 MHz to 30 GHz. At low frequency the collector additionally resonates: `Lchoke` is only j110 ohm at 176 MHz -- not a choke there -- and the load it forms with the array capacitance and the port peaks the gain at 29 dB around 176 MHz, where mu bottoms.

The fix is four ideal resistors (no inductor added, DR 0001 D-1 open): `Rin` (series at RFIN) and `Rshm` (shunt at the `Cin` tap node) pad the input and cut the loop product in both directions; `Rsho` (shunt at RFOUT) gives the 10-30 MHz output port the resistive loss it otherwise lacks (Cblock makes it a near-lossless reactance there, |S22| ≈ 1.0); `Rdchoke` (across `Lchoke`) damps the bias-feed resonance that peaks the gain below the band. The cost is in-band gain (see the ac-gain record of this design revision).
