### Observations on the pre-#172 instability, and what changed

Before the issue-#172 network (`records/20261118-225926-ef3c005.md`, same cell), mu was 0.0228 at 0.176 GHz and below 1 at **every** one of the 174 points; k was also below 1 throughout, while max |delta|, max |S11| and max |S22| were all below 1 (no negative-resistance port). What the pre-change run measured at the quiescent point (ngspice operating point, emitter grounded): V_CE = 0.8 V (the collector node sits at the supply) and the base at 0.778 V, so V_CB = +21.97 mV -- the base-collector junction is reverse-biased by only ~22 mV, i.e. near zero bias, not forward-biased. The archived vectors give a loop product |S12*S21| of 0.744 at 176.25 MHz and 0.0601 at 30 GHz. At low frequency the collector additionally resonates: `Lchoke` is only j110 ohm at 176 MHz -- not a choke there -- and the load it forms with the array capacitance and the port peaks the gain around 176 MHz, where mu bottoms. These are observations from the vectors and the log; no device-level mechanism is asserted beyond them.

The fix is four ideal resistors (no inductor added, DR 0001 D-1 open): `Rin` (series at RFIN) and `Rshm` (shunt at the `Cin` tap node) pad the input and cut the loop product in both directions; `Rsho` (shunt at RFOUT) gives the 10-30 MHz output port the resistive loss it otherwise lacks (Cblock makes it a near-lossless reactance there, |S22| ≈ 1.0); `Rdchoke` (across `Lchoke`) damps the bias-feed resonance that peaks the gain below the band. The cost is in-band gain (see the ac-gain record of this design revision).
